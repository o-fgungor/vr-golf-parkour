# VR Golf Parkour

A VR locomotion study built in Unity for Meta Quest, developed as part of the Interaction in Virtual and Augmented Reality (IVAR) course at TU Darmstadt, winter semester 2025/2026. The player navigates a parkour course by hitting a golf ball with a physical club, when the ball stops, they teleport to its position. The goal is to complete the course while collecting coins and interacting with objects along the way.
